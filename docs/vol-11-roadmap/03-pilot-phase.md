# Chapter 03 — Pilot Phase (Year 1–2)

## The Pilot Objective

The pilot phase proves the G.O.D.S ecosystem in real-world conditions with real users and real governance decisions. It is not a demonstration or a proof of concept — it is a production deployment serving a real client, with real constitutional obligations.

The pilot is designed to validate:
1. The governance architecture works under real load and real data
2. The EVA scoring and PolicyPack produce appropriate outcomes in practice
3. The SETHS employment pipeline creates measurable value for learners and employers
4. The audit chain holds up under real governance activity
5. The POPIA and Employment Equity Act compliance obligations are met

---

## Pilot Scope

### Year 1 Focus: SETHS and UDOC

The pilot concentrates on two divisions:

**SETHS Pilot:**
- 1 pilot employer (verified, willing to participate)
- 100–500 learner registrations
- 20–50 active opportunities
- Full governance path on all employment decisions
- Employment equity reporting to the pilot employer

**UDOC Pilot:**
- 1–3 AI operators (organisations using AI in employment decisions)
- Full model registration and governance path
- SaaS client experience (operator portal, API integration)
- Oversight case workflow with real compliance officers

### Year 2 Addition: MADIBA

Once SETHS and UDOC are stable, add:
- 3–5 pilot projects in the MADIBA pipeline
- 1–2 institutional investor participants
- Milestone tracking in a real capital deployment context

## Pilot Governance Structure

The pilot has a formal governance structure:

**Pilot Oversight Board:**
- Sashin J. Singh (founder / platform operator)
- 1 representative from the pilot employer
- 1 independent governance advisor
- Quarterly reviews

**Compliance Officer:**
- 1 designated compliance officer for the pilot period
- Responsible for reviewing all ESCALATE and BLOCK outcomes
- Responsible for PolicyPack review and approval

**Sovereign Audit:**
- Monthly audit chain integrity verification
- Quarterly governance outcome review
- Findings reported to the Oversight Board

---

## The Honest Pilot Assessment

At the end of Year 2, the Oversight Board produces an honest assessment:

1. **What worked:** Governance mechanisms that performed as designed
2. **What didn't:** Outcomes that were unexpected, incorrect, or impractical
3. **What needs changing:** PolicyPack adjustments, EVA weight recalibrations, UX improvements
4. **Constitutional soundness:** Did any constitutional check need to be refined?

The pilot assessment is published in `00_ECOSYSTEM_STATUS.md` and informs the regional expansion decision (Chapter 04).
