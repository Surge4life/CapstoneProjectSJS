## 1. The natural story

The Capstone did not start as “train a model.”  
It started as **process of thought**: human primacy, sovereign control, and the refusal to let fluent generation replace accountable decision.

That thinking was written into doctrine, manifesto, and founder documents — the **book** layer.

Those documents were then **structured** into the GIS Intelligence Corpus (`10_GIS_INTELLIGENCE_CORPUS`, folders 00–15): governance, legal, finance, strategy, UDOC, divisions, GBS, architecture, builds, website, branding, Capstone evidence, method.

From that structure comes the **GODS Language Model (GLM)**:

> **GLM = Deterministic Human-In-The-Loop Governance Model — not an LLM.**

| Layer | Role | Controller? |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **A** — Optional generative assist (any LLM) | Draft, retrieve-explain, outside-GODS fluency | **No** |
| **B** — UDOC / EVA / policy-to-code | Fail-closed decisions, audit, HITL | **Yes** |
| **C** — GIS corpus + pilot extracts | Knowledge substrate | Feeds B only |

Client intelligence and continued corpus build are how this stack **steers** generative tools over time. They do not replace the control plane. Long-horizon design (constitutional governance across generations) is the **mandate framing**, not a claim that Capstone already ships a 250-year system.

## 3. Integrity of scale

- **Now:** pilot extracts + rules + live EVA demonstrate the method under Neon/Render limits.  
- **When funded / hardware upgrades:** same hierarchy ingests deeper corpus text; control plane does not invert.  
- **If scale never arrives:** the groundwork still shows how governance intelligence **should** be done — corpus first, deterministic evaluation second, generative tools last.

Neon holds **citations and short extracts**, not the full corpus. Drive holds the corpus. Local disk may hold offline index for pilot. That split is intentional.
