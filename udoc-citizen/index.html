Welcome to UDOC Citizen Portal

This is your place to challenge AI decisions that affected you. You have rights. We help you use them.

YOUR RIGHTS UNDER SOUTH AFRICAN LAW
If an AI system made a decision about you (like denying a grant, rejecting a job application, or flagging your tax return), you have the right to: receive an explanation, challenge the decision, and have a human review your case.

You have the right to know WHY an AI made a decision about you. (Section 33 RSA Constitution)

Example Explanation — Welfare Grant Decision

What the AI decided: Your application was flagged for human review.

Why the AI flagged it:
• Income below the threshold (helps your case — 42%)
• Disability documentation — could not verify documents (hurt your case — 31%)
• Time unemployed — 18 months (helps — 18%)
• Grant payment history — always complied (helps — 9%)

What this means: If disability documentation were fully verified, the system indicates you would likely have been APPROVED with high confidence.

What happens next: A human reviewer should contact you. Keep your reference number.

Your Rights When AI is Used

Under the RSA Constitution and related instruments, you have specific rights when AI makes a decision about you. GG54477 was withdrawn 26 Apr 2026; live basis includes POPIA and just administrative action.
